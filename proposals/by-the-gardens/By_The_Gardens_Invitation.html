The story of Arca South

A 74-hectare estate, fourteen years in the making

Arca South began as the government’s Food Terminal Inc. site in Taguig. Today it is Ayala Land’s next estate: between Makati, BGC and Alabang, with C-5 frontage and SLEX access. Two national rail lines are being built into it.
