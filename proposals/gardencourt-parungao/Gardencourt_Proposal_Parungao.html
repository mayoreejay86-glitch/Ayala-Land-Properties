Arca South: Investment and Infrastructure

Arca South is a 74-hectare Ayala Land estate on the former Food Terminal Inc. (FTI) site in Taguig, between Makati, BGC and Alabang, with frontage on C-5 and access to SLEX. Government-funded rail lines and a regional transport hub are being built inside and beside the estate.

Definitions

Rest of down completes the down payment (cash: full amount within 30 days). Monthly is fixed for the term. Balance is a lump sum, usually paid through a bank loan. Other charges cover title transfer taxes and fees, utility deposits and two years of condo dues.
